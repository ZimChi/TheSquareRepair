Rotted deck joist replacement near 29 North and Proffit Road:

Complete framing and Fiberon decking

Problem: A homeowner in the Proffit Road and 29 North area reported that the deck bent downward when stepped on. Investigation found that the supporting joists were severely deteriorated and structurally compromised. The existing railing also needed to be preserved while the damaged framing was removed.

Repair: The existing deck structure was carefully dismantled, including the decking boards, deteriorated joists, band board, and ledger board. Temporary bracing preserved the railing while new weatherproofing and framing were installed. A new butyl barrier was applied against the house, with Z-bar flashing over the new 2x10 ledger board and Lexel sealant used where appropriate. New 16-foot 2x10 joists were installed 12 inches on center with joist hangers, and butyl tape was applied across the tops of the joists. The original railing was integrated into the new framing with carriage bolts to replicate the original installation. Fiberon decking was then installed with hidden fasteners.
