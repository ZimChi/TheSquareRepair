Front Porch Column Rot Repair Near Albemarle Square Charlottesville VA

Problem: The wooden base of this front-porch column had rotted where it met the concrete base. The rot went through the base molding and into the bottom of the column.

Repair: I removed the deteriorated wood and the broken base molding, then rebuilt the base and reshaped the damaged bottom of the column using Bondo polyester wood filler. Once the repair was shaped and sanded, I primed it and painted the column to match the others on the porch. A few of the other columns had smaller areas of damage and received a lesser amount of Bondo repair as well.
