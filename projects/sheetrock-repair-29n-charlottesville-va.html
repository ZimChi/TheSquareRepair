Sheetrock Paper Tear Repair in Charlottesville VA

Problem: Three spots on a bathroom wall where the paper facing had torn off, leaving bare brown paper exposed. The damaged areas could not be painted over without treatment — the raw paper would absorb moisture from the paint and cause visible bubbling or peeling.

Repair: The torn paper areas were smoothed over with multiple coats of joint compound, applied in thin layers and sanded between each coat, until the damaged spots were built flush with the surrounding wall surface. The repaired areas were then painted to match.
